Every Estonian race,
every result, one place.

Estonia has excellent timekeepers. The problem is that there are many of them, and none of them knows about the others. After a race you know your time is published somewhere — you just don't know where. So you search, you guess, you dig through a Facebook group, and sometimes you never find it at all.

LostTimes solves exactly that and nothing else.

How it works

Every night a script reads the event listings of Estonia's timekeepers and collects one thing from each: the link to the results. Where a timekeeper doesn't have them, it follows the organiser's own page one step further.

  • We don't host results. Every link takes you to the timekeeper's own page.
  • We store no personal data. Only race name, date and a link — no names, no times.
  • No account, no login, no cookie banner. You came to see a time, not to register.

Sources

ChampionChip, Sportos, timing.ee, Estoloppet, Antrotsenter, TolkNet (via racetecresults) and organisers' own pages.

LostTimes is where Estonian runners, cyclists, triathletes and skiers go to find their race results — usually within an hour of finishing.

Who is here

Amateur endurance athletes. People who pay entry fees, own gear, and race repeatedly through the season.
They arrive with intent. Nobody browses a results site. They come looking for one specific thing, having just done the sport.
They come back. A typical amateur races 5–15 times a year, and looks up results after every one.
They are in a buying frame of mind. The hour after a race is when people think about the next entry, better shoes, a bike fit, a training plan.

Placements

Race list placement

A single sponsored row inside the results list, in the same format as everything else — no banner, no popup. Seen by everyone who opens the site.

Upcoming races sponsorship

Your brand on the Upcoming tab, where people look when they are deciding what to enter next. The natural place for events, entry platforms and travel.

Season or sport partnership

For longer commitments: presence across the site through a full season, with first refusal on new placements as they appear.
